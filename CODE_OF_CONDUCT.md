# Code of Conduct

MORPH follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexual language or imagery, or personal attacks, in any project
  space: issues, pull requests, discussions and chat.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/yahyeameer/MORPH/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting a maintainer listed on the repository. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may remove content and warn, suspend or ban participants, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
